# San Francisco Coverage Roadmap

Date: 2026-03-13

## Purpose
Build `San Francisco` as the second flagship US Alleycat pack after New York.

Status: planned

## Why San Francisco
- core fixed-gear city in US culture
- strong hill / bridge / waterfront route identity
- very different riding logic than New York
- good stress test for spread, elevation feel, and district balance

## Scope
- target `24 to 36` curated checkpoints for launch
- cover:
  - SoMa
  - Mission
  - Tenderloin edge
  - North Beach
  - Embarcadero seams
  - Dogpatch / Potrero edge
  - Western Addition / Hayes seam
  - bridge and waterfront entries where useful
- avoid tourist-only postcard filler
- keep tasks short, sharp, and rider-coded

## Rollout role
- follows New York
- becomes the second template for US flagship city packs
- then informs future US rollouts like Chicago and Philadelphia

## Success condition
- San Francisco feels distinct from New York
- routes use real hill / bridge / waterfront logic
- the pack stays dense enough for smaller spreads
