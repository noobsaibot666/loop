# New York Coverage Roadmap

Date: 2026-03-13

## Purpose
Build a special Alleycat pack for `New York` that is meaningfully deeper than the current city packs.

This is not just "add one more city."

New York should become:
- the flagship Alleycat city
- the deepest checkpoint library in the app
- the benchmark for future US city rollouts

Status: planned

## Why New York first
- brand fit is obvious
- messenger culture roots are strongest here
- riders will expect better coverage than a normal city pack
- it gives us the right model for `San Francisco`, `Chicago`, `Philadelphia`, and other US fixed-gear cities later

## Scope

### 1. Large checkpoint library
New York should ship with broad and useful checkpoint coverage.

Target:
- `35 to 50` curated checkpoints for launch

Coverage goals:
- Manhattan
- Brooklyn
- Queens
- key bridge / waterfront seams
- a few out-island or edge cases where they make sense

Avoid:
- tourist-only postcard picks
- Times Square style filler unless the task is genuinely useful
- too many repeated downtown landmarks

### 2. District spread
The city should not collapse into one neighborhood.

Target districts / lanes:
- Lower Manhattan
- Midtown / west side seams
- LES / Chinatown / East Village
- Williamsburg
- Greenpoint
- Bushwick
- Downtown Brooklyn
- Fort Greene / Clinton Hill
- Long Island City / Astoria
- bridge entries and waterfront seams

### 3. Better task tone
New York tasks need to feel sharper than generic packs.

Rules:
- street-first, not tourist-first
- short, direct, and rider-coded
- funny where it helps, never corny
- real route pressure and neighborhood feel

### 4. Pack behavior
New York should work well across a wider range of start areas and spreads.

Target:
- better support for tight Manhattan / Brooklyn starts
- better neighborhood density
- stronger district balancing during manifest generation

### 5. Admin review support
Because the pack will be larger, City Studio needs a cleaner review flow for it.

Target improvements:
- borough / district grouping
- spot count by district
- denser preview for large packs
- easier publish confidence checks

## Rollout order

### Wave A
- New York flagship pack

### Wave B
- San Francisco

### Wave C
- next US fixed-gear cities after live feedback
- likely candidates:
  - Chicago
  - Philadelphia
  - Los Angeles
  - Seattle

## Guardrails
- keep AI review-gated
- do not auto-publish drafts
- do not bloat the public product copy around the city
- keep mobile-first scanning intact

## Success condition
This expansion is successful if:
- New York feels like the special Alleycat city in the app
- manifests can pull useful routes across major NYC lanes
- the pack avoids tourist sludge
- the same process becomes reusable for San Francisco and future US cities
