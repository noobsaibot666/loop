# Alleycat City Rollout

Date: 2026-03-09

## Purpose
Define a practical rollout plan for Alleycat city packs so the product grows with quality control instead of turning into a tourism generator.

## City selection rule
- Choose cities with visible fixed-gear, messenger, or alleycat energy.
- Prefer cities with district variety and strong street identity.
- Avoid trying to launch too many packs at once.
- Use AI to draft copy and pack tone, but keep checkpoint selection and publish approval human-reviewed.

## Rollout waves

### Wave 1
- Berlin
- London
- Tokyo

Why:
- strong identity
- high route variety
- clear alleycat / fixed-gear fit
- enough contrast to stress-test the tooling

### Wave 2
- Mexico City
- Bogotá
- Warsaw

Why:
- strong street-riding energy
- good challenge tone potential
- less obvious tourism-first feel when packs are built correctly

### Wave 3
- Barcelona
- São Paulo
- Buenos Aires
- New York City

Why:
- culturally strong
- should be validated carefully before publishing
- better once the admin flow and content review system are less manual

## Content rules for every city pack
- No tourism loops.
- Do not build packs around postcard landmarks by default.
- Push packs across districts instead of clustering everything in the center.
- Favor underpasses, plazas, bridges, side streets, markets, basketball courts, train edges, skate-adjacent zones, riversides, and weird local corners.
- Task copy should feel street-smart, funny, direct, and a little shameless.
- Keep tasks safe and doable in live city riding conditions.
- No harassment, trespassing, vandalism, or dangerous riding prompts.

## Task tone targets
- `Local`
  - observant, rooted, city-specific
- `Fast`
  - dispatch pressure, quick decisions, moving on
- `Chaotic`
  - playful, awkward, funny, mildly embarrassing, but still safe

## Good task directions
- weird observation
- tiny ritual
- short street dare
- pop-culture prompt linked to the spot
- micro challenge to do with a friend or camera
- local detail most tourists would miss

## Bad task directions
- monument worship
- generic sightseeing
- “take in the view”
- shopping / food-tour behavior
- anything that sounds like a city guide blog

## Admin workflow per city
1. Create the pack shell.
2. Draft route tone and finish label with AI.
3. Add 8-15 checkpoints with district/category/vibe data.
4. Draft checkpoint tasks with AI.
5. Edit tasks manually until they read like a real alleycat.
6. Preview the manifest.
7. Publish only after route spread and tone feel right.
