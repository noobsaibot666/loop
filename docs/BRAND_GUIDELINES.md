# Loop Brand Guidelines

## 1. Vision & Tone
Loop is a premium coordination platform for bike messengers and night-riders. The brand should feel **fast, reliable, and "after-dark" professional**.

- **Keywords**: Precision, Flow, Emerald, Obsidian, Hybrid.
- **Tone of Voice**: Direct, trustworthy, and sleek.

## 2. Visual Identity

### Color Palette
- **Obsidian Black**: `#0A0A0A` (Backgrounds, primary UI)
- **Loop Emerald**: `#00FF99` (Primary accents, success states, glowing paths)
- **Mercury Silver**: `#E0E0E0` (Primary text, high-contrast lines)
- **Deep Slate**: `#1A1A1A` (Component backgrounds, subtle borders)

### Typography
- **Primary**: *Inter* or *Roboto* (Clean, legible sans-serif for high-speed use).
- **Accents**: Monospace for data points and credit balances to emphasize precision.

### Iconography
- All icons should be minimal and high-contrast.
- Use glowing effects sparsely to highlight active "Loops" or paths.

## 3. Logo & Assets
The primary brand mark is a stylized infinite loop with integrated circuit-style paths, representing the flow of city coordination.
