Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 14, 2026

Hard Chain ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Hard Chain mobile application. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

  • Email address — used for account creation and authentication.
  • User IDs — internal identifiers used to manage your account and ride activity.
  • Precise and approximate location — collected while the app is in use to validate checkpoints and calculate ride distance. Not collected in the background.
  • Photos — uploaded by you as ride proof or Night Ride posts.
  • Camera — accessed to capture proof photos and Night Ride shots directly in the app.
  • Purchase history — records of in-app purchases made through the app.
  • App interactions — how you use app features, used to improve performance.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — collected automatically to identify and fix bugs.
  • Device IDs — used for analytics, fraud prevention, and security.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the app and provide ride, checkpoint, and wall features.
  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To process in-app purchases.
  • To monitor app performance and fix crashes.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the following third-party service providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Supabase — cloud database and authentication infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management.
  • Stripe — payment processing.

These providers process data solely to deliver their services and are bound by their own privacy policies.

4. User-Generated Content

Wall posts and ride proofs you submit are visible to other users of the app. Profile fields are only public where explicitly indicated. You can control what you share by not filling in optional fields.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, visit our support page or contact us directly. All user data will be permanently removed from our systems upon request.

7. Children's Privacy

Hard Chain is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
